# Question bank expansion roadmap

## Current seed bank
The website currently ships with 35 source-referenced questions spanning:
- Poverty concepts and poverty measurement
- Children's Future Education and Development Account
- Low-income and middle-low-income household eligibility
- Social Assistance Act, benefits, workfare, medical and emergency assistance, and anti-poverty policy

## Editorial standards for future question sets

1. **Source-first**: Every answer must be supported by the uploaded lecture PDF. Include page references.
2. **Hard, not ambiguous**: Make distractors plausible by changing one important criterion, time period, threshold, responsible authority, or exception. Do not create multiple defensible answers.
3. **Mix cognitive levels**:
   - Recall: exact term, article, eligibility threshold.
   - Discrimination: compare closely related concepts or methods.
   - Application: apply rules to a household vignette.
   - Calculation: household income per person per month, thresholds, and account contributions.
   - Synthesis: combine two or more lecture concepts while keeping the answer fully supported.
4. **Explain distractors**: Good rationales should identify why the correct answer fits and why the tempting alternatives fail.
5. **Respect lecture scope**: If the slide deck does not specify an answer, flag the gap rather than silently importing outside law or knowledge.
6. **Version-sensitive rules**: Clearly mark any amounts, ages, or statutory wording that depend on the lecture's date/version.

## Roadmap
- Expand every major section of the 144-page lecture, not just the initial sample set.
- Add multi-select and short-answer formats only after the single-answer multiple-choice bank is robust.
- Add spaced repetition and a missed-concept dashboard.
- Consider optional cloud sync only if a secure backend is introduced; the current version intentionally stores user data locally.
